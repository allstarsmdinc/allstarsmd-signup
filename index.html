📌 Before You Sign Up — Please Read

AllstarsMD, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Every participant is a volunteer. In return for your time, we make a donation to the organization of your choice on behalf of the youth or young adult you are fundraising for.

Our revenue comes from a percentage of sales we generate at our assigned stand, plus our collective tips.

A $35 mandatory annual donation is required per fundraising season, beginning July 1st.

⚠️ You must arrive at the designated time. If anyone in our group is late, the stadium assesses a fee against the entire organization. Please do not sign up for an event you cannot fully commit to.

⚠️ No-shows hurt the whole group. If you sign up and do not appear, AllstarsMD is subject to a fee that affects everyone's earnings. No-shows may be removed from the program and forfeit any pending fundraising earnings.
